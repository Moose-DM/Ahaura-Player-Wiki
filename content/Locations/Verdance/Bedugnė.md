## Overview
Bedugnė is a city built into the caldera of an ancient volcano. While the volcano erupted long ago, there is enough geothermal heat left over to cause the lake in the center of the city, Hell's Iris, to be boiling hot. Crime runs rampant here, so tread lightly. You only ever find people in Bedugnė for two reasons, they are conducting non-kosher business, or they can't claw their way out.
