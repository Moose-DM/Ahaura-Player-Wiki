# Overview
Helio is the capital city of Verdance. It is carved into the peak of Mt. Sol, the highest mountain top in all of Ahaura, making it the closest point to the radiant light of the Sun God [[Zenith]].  For the past 30 years the city has been a utopia, free of crime and death from violence. This is partly attributed to the ban on all arcane magic. It is guarded by the legendary [[TORCH Knights]]. There is spiral road carved into the mountain top that allows for easy trade with the rest of Verdance.
