## Overview
Calchester is a farming village, and home to a popular Winter Solstice Festival! This festival celebrates the start of the days getting longer, which means more sunlight, and crops. 

## Attributes
During the festival, the market square transforms. The center tent showcases shows, and artists, like [[Clovis Featherbottom - Pat|Clovis]], are hired to paint the festival goes in various styles.

## Rumors and Happenings
The [[Black Foot]] is kidnapping people from town to sell as sacrifices.
