- The party made their way to the [[The Black Foot]] base camp
- On the way [[Cornelius]] caught up with the party and gave them healing potions. [[Thurnador - Thomas|Thurnador]] also gave him a heartfelt apology for his previous actions.
- Randy Smokes introduced them to [[One Eyed Windham]]
- [[One Eyed Windham]] told the party that the recent kidnapping events have been against [[The Black Foot | The Black Foot's]] will. They usually only steal money
- A skeletal figure approach the bandit leader and told her she needed to provide him with human sacrifices
- [[One Eyed Windham]] agreed to help the skeleton man fearing what might happen if she went against his wishes
- The party waited for the skeleton to come and retrieve his next batch of prisoners and tailed him to his lair
- The party entered a ghost town, and made their way down the abandoned well
- [[Puuglar - Bart|Puuglar]], afraid of the water, closed his eyes and held onto [[Thurnador - Thomas|Thurnador's]] cloak
- The party dispatched two bone crabs, [[Puuglar - Bart|Puuglar]] kept his eyes closed but still managed to help
- The party ventured further into the lair and approached a patch of poison mushroom
- [[Puuglar - Bart|Puuglar]] managed to tip toe his way around the blistering shrooms and found an alchemist kidnapped by the skeletons
- The alchemist's name was Jarrad, and rewarded the party with a few more healing potions for helping him escape
- He has been using the mushrooms to make a knockout compound, which he gave a vial of to [[Puuglar - Bart|Puuglar]]
- He also warned the party of an evil spirit on the other side of the mushroom cave
- The party then pushed forward and found three cages, holding the people they followed here
- Everybody in the cage was brought here today, so whatever the skeleton's were going to do, it was going to happen soon
- The skeleton's prodded the people and said that soon they would be rid of their impure fresh, and made clean they had been
- The party quickly dispatched the three skeletons and freed the prisoners, who went back to the well entrance to make their escape with the alchemist
