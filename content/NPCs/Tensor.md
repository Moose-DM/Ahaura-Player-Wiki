# Overview
Tensor was a wizard known for pushing the boundary of magic. He was a member of the legendary [[Lightbringers]], and was known just as much for charging into the frontline as he was for his powerful ranged magic. After the defeat of [[Oros]] and distrust of arcane magic, Tensor stepped away from adventuring.
