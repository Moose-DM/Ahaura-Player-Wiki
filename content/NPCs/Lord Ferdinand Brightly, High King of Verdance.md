# Overview
Lord Ferdinand Brightly is the High King of Verdance, a title granted to the ruler of the capital city of [[Helio]]. Lord Ferdinand is accredited with ushering an era of peace after the [[Lightbringers]] vanquished the evil necromancer [[Oros]]. Under his rule the city of [[Helio]] has flourished and the people of Verdance have a newfound sense of hope. He is loved by the people of Verdance, and respected all throughout Ahaura as a benevolent King.

He rules along side his wife [[Queen Isabelle]] whom he shares a son with, [[Prince Mateo Brightly]].
