# Overview
The legendary Lightbringers! This band of heroes are looked up to by many across Ahaura for vanquishing the evil necromancer [[Oros]]. They were disbanded after this feat because of the death of their leader [[Sir Escanor le Grand]] and the growing distain for arcane magic.
